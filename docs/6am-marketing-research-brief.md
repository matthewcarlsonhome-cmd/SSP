### SKILL 1: Campaign Strategy Builder
**SkillEngine ID:** `campaign-strategy-builder`
**Maps to 6AM Service:** Strategy

**The Problem:** Every new client campaign begins with a blank strategy document. The strategy team spends 8-12 hours crafting objectives, audience definitions, channel recommendations, and success metrics from scratch -- even when patterns repeat across similar campaigns.

**What SkillEngine Delivers:**
- Complete campaign strategy document with objectives, positioning, and channel mix
- Audience segmentation and targeting recommendations
- Channel allocation strategy with budget recommendations
- Success metrics framework and measurement plan
- Competitive positioning for the campaign

**Demo Scenario for 6AM:** "Imagine Healthfirst comes to you with a new patient acquisition campaign for a clinic opening in Dane County. Instead of starting from scratch, your strategist inputs the target audience, geographic focus, competitive landscape, and budget -- and within minutes gets a comprehensive strategy document they can refine and present. That 8-hour strategy session becomes a 2-hour refinement session."

**Estimated Time Saved:** 6-8 hours per campaign
**Annual Impact:** 15-20 campaigns/year = 90-160 hours saved

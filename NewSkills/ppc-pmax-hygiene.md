## PMax Fundamentals for Pool/Spa Lead Gen

### What PMax Actually Does
One campaign serves across Search, Shopping, Display, YouTube, Gmail, and Maps using Google's algorithm to find converting users. For pool/spa lead gen:
- **Works well**: Finding in-market homeowners who haven't yet searched directly
- **Risk**: Optimizing for cheap "conversions" that are actually low-intent (DIY research, renters, etc.)
- **Your job**: Feed it good assets, set strong guardrails, document what it's doing

### The Core Risk: Quality vs. Quantity
PMax will hit your conversion targets — but it may fill them with garbage leads if you let it. The guardrails below are non-negotiable.

## PMax Guardrails — Non-Negotiable Setup

Verify these exist on every PMax account. If missing, submit brief to internal team:

### 1. Brand Keyword Exclusions
Add brand terms as negative keywords at the campaign level (via support request or account manager — PMax doesn't have a standard negative KW interface):
- Client's own brand name
- Common misspellings
- "[Brand] + location" combinations
**Why**: Without this, PMax absorbs branded search traffic you'd win for $0.50 CPC and spends $8+ CPC on it.

### 2. URL Exclusions
Ensure PMax isn't sending traffic to:
- Contact/thank you pages (creates circular conversion counting)
- Blog posts or educational content
- Team/about pages (not conversion-oriented)
- Any page that doesn't have the primary CTA

**How to add**: Campaign Settings → URL Expansion → Exclude specific URLs

### 3. Placement Exclusions (Display/YouTube)
Add these at the account level:
- Parked domains
- Mobile app categories (games, utilities)
- YouTube channels irrelevant to homeowners/pool shoppers

### 4. Conversion Action Priority
Verify PMax is optimizing for the RIGHT conversion action:
- ✅ Form submission (qualified lead)
- ✅ Phone call (60+ seconds)
- ❌ Page views
- ❌ Session duration
- ❌ Button clicks (unless confirmed lead-intent buttons)

## Asset Group Structure Best Practices

### Recommended Structure for Pool/Spa Accounts

**Asset Group 1: Pool Installation / Construction**
- Theme: In-ground pools, new construction, custom pools
- Audience signals: In-market for Pool & Spa Services, Custom Home Construction interest, homeowners 35-65
- Headlines should include: "Custom In-Ground Pools," "Free Design Consultation," local city name
- Images: completed pool installations, family enjoying pool, aerial/glamour shots

**Asset Group 2: Pool Service / Maintenance (if applicable)**
- Theme: Ongoing service, chemical care, cleaning
- Audience signals: Existing pool owners, home services interest
- Headlines: "Weekly Pool Service," "Professional Pool Care," "Licensed Pool Technicians"
- Images: technician working, clean blue water, service truck

**Asset Group 3: Hot Tub / Spa (if applicable)**
- Theme: Spas, hot tubs, hydrotherapy
- Audience signals: Hot tub interest, home wellness, backyard renovation
- Headlines: "In-Ground Spa Installation," "Hot Tub + Pool Combos," "Custom Spa Design"
- Images: spa at night with lighting, couple relaxing, spa with waterfall feature

## Asset Performance Review Process

### Step 1: Access Asset Reporting
Google Ads → Your PMax Campaign → Asset Groups → View assets

Asset performance ratings:
- **Best** = Keep, identify what makes it work
- **Good** = Keep, monitor
- **Low** = Flag for replacement
- **Learning** = New, needs 2–3 more weeks before judging

### Step 2: Prune Low-Performing Assets
**Prune when:**
- Asset has been live 4+ weeks AND rated "Low" consistently
- Asset is conceptually misaligned with the asset group theme

**Never prune down to minimum counts:**
- Headlines: minimum 3 required, keep 8–10
- Descriptions: minimum 2 required, keep 4–5
- Images: minimum 1 required, keep 5–10
- Videos: optional but recommended — if no video, Google auto-generates one (usually bad)

### Step 3: Submit Image Briefs to Design Team

When flagging image needs, use this brief format:
```
ACCOUNT: [Name]
ASSET GROUP: [Name]
IMAGE NEED: [Type: lifestyle / product / proof / CTA overlay]
DESCRIPTION: [What should be in the image — specific, visual]
DIMENSIONS NEEDED: Landscape (1.91:1), Square (1:1), Portrait (4:5)
DEADLINE: [When needed by]
REFERENCE: [Link to example if available]
EXAMPLE: "Backyard pool with family, evening lighting, warm colors, 
          no text overlay — show the aspiration of having a pool"
```

### Step 4: Audience Signal Optimization

Audience signals tell PMax where to start — not where to stop. Review monthly:

**Strong signals for pool/spa lead gen:**
- Google Audiences: "In-market: Pool & Spa Services," "In-market: Home Improvement," "Life Events: Home purchase"
- Custom intent: people who searched [competitor names], [pool installation queries], [financing pools]
- Customer list: upload existing client list if available (creates similar audience)

**Add, don't replace:** Layer signals together — removing a signal that's working can cause performance drops.

## Detecting PMax Cannibalization

**Signs PMax is stealing from Search:**
- Search campaign impression share drops while PMax is running
- Branded search conversions drop on exact match campaigns
- Search CPL rises while PMax CPL looks artificially low

**How to investigate:**
1. Compare Search campaign performance week-over-week vs. PMax launch date
2. Check "Auction Insights" on Search campaigns — if your own ads appear less often, PMax may be absorbing queries
3. Pull a Placement Report from PMax (Reporting → Predefined Reports → Extensions → Placement) to see where budget is going

**Fix:**
- Ensure Search campaigns have all high-intent keywords as EXACT match (PMax won't compete with exact match in same account in theory, but it still happens)
- Add brand exclusions to PMax
- Consider separating brand and non-brand into separate budgets

## PMax vs. Demand Gen: When to Use What

| Scenario | Use PMax | Use Demand Gen |
|----------|----------|---------------|
| Primary conversion goal | ✅ | ❌ |
| Account has 30+ conversions/month | ✅ | Optional add-on |
| Account has <15 conversions/month | ⚠️ Risky | ❌ |
| Remarketing to warm visitors | ⚠️ Can't control | ✅ |
| Spring awareness push (pre-season) | ⚠️ Supplemental | ✅ |
| Brand new market/audience | ❌ | ✅ |

**For most agency pool/spa accounts:** PMax as primary (with strong guardrails) + optional Demand Gen for remarketing only.
